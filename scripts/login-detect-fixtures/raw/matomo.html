Here's what's new in Matomo

FormAnalytics - Create Custom Reports for your forms

Form Name and Form ID are now available as dimensions in Custom Reports, so you can combine your form analytics data with any metrics and dimensions you like.

Cloud - Cohorts and Funnels now available as dashboard widgets!

You can now embed Cohorts and Funnels reports directly into your Matomo dashboard for quicker access to retention and conversion metrics. These updates provide a more flexible way to track user behaviour over time and optimise key journeys.

Funnels - New: All your funnels in one clear view

The Funnels Overview report now includes a Funnels Summary table that aggregates key funnel metrics, including entries and conversion rates. This makes it easier to analyse performance across multiple funnels in one view.
